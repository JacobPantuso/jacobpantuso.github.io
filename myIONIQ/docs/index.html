Overview

myIONIQ is an iPhone, CarPlay and Apple Watch app for Hyundai and Kia e-GMP EVs, such as the IONIQ 5, IONIQ 6 and EV6. It talks to the car three ways:

SourceWorks FromUsed For
BluelinkAnywhereRemote commands (lock, climate, charging, horn and lights) and the car's last reported status
OBD-II adapterAt the carLive charge, real charge power, locks and doors, trips and regen; required for CarPlay
Harness adapterAt the carBattery preconditioning and CarPlay directions on the cluster and HUD

Only Bluelink is required. Each adapter adds to it, and the app works out which source has the freshest answer for every reading.

Status

Early accessmyIONIQ is in a TestFlight beta. It's built and tested on an IONIQ 6 with a Canadian Bluelink account. Other e-GMP cars and regions may work, but haven't been tested yet, and reports from them are especially useful.

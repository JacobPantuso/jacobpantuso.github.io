Release Notes

What's new in each version of Numen.

Version 1.0

October 30, 2026 · Build 20261030.1642

New

  • Share Vehicle. Share your car with the household from Settings › Vehicle › Share Vehicle. Everyone’s trips and charges sync into one history. Your Bluelink sign-in is never shared.
  • ALPHA-TESTING Unlock through the WiCAN. Unlock goes to the harness WiCAN over Bluetooth first, then Bluelink — so it’s faster, and works even when Bluelink is down.
  • A faster charger map. Chargers in Navigate cluster as you zoom out, and tapping one shows its connectors, charge time to 80%, network, spaces, cost and status.
  • ALPHA-TESTING Learn Vehicle Boundaries. Replaces Set From Where You’re Standing in Settings › Auto-Lock: walk a lap around the car and Numen sets the Walk-Away Lock distance just past its weakest spot.
  • ALPHA-TESTING Updating the WiCAN from the app. Updates now show each step — Installing, Verifying, Restarting — and when it was last updated. If your WiCAN’s firmware is too old to update from the app, Numen lets you know and links to the latest release.
  • CarPlay: the Dashboard always draws the live route line.

Fixed

  • Walk-Away Lock could lock while you walked around the front of the car. It now holds longer and ignores brief signal dips.
  • The charging Live Activity could stick around after the charge ended. (#30)
  • The 12V battery row showed the main battery’s charge. (#32)
  • Car status refreshed too slowly while the app was open. (#33)
  • Fetch Vehicle Status in Shortcuts could time out instead of answering with the last status. (#34)
  • Shortcuts and the Watch reported the car as unauthenticated after force-quitting Numen. (#35)
  • Climate started from a widget or the Watch used the car’s default temperature instead of your last settings. (#36)
  • Some trips didn’t appear in Recents until the next launch. (#37)
  • A “not signed in” error when a command ran while Numen was signing back in. (#38)
  • Walking back to the car mid-charge could split one charge into two.
  • Trip Planner’s charging-stop bar counted stops where you didn’t charge.
  • CarPlay: the Dashboard pin could sit off the route line, and arrival now reads “Arrived”.
  • Cluster/HUD: cleaner left and right arrows on one-lane roads. (#24)

October 26, 2026 · Build 20261026.1000

Thank you for all the bug reports this week. Most of this build came straight from them.

New

  • Update your WiCAN from Numen. When a firmware update is ready, tap Update Adapter in Settings › OBD Adapter. It takes about a minute. Heads up: your WiCAN needs the latest Numen firmware (numen-2026.10.25 or newer) before it can receive updates from the app. If you flashed an earlier Numen build, or haven’t flashed it yet, install the latest Numen firmware release from the WiCAN’s local web page first, and every update after that comes through the app.
  • Vehicle Location widget. See where your car is on a small map, with the address and whether it’s parked or driving. Available on the Home Screen and Lock Screen.
  • Climate duration. Choose how long climate runs (5, 10, 15, 20 or 30 minutes) in Quick Start. Presets, Siri, widgets and the Watch all use it, and the Live Activity counts down to match.
  • Walk-Away Lock distance. Pick Short, Medium or Long in Settings › Auto-Lock, or tap Set From Where You’re Standing to use your own spot.
  • Surround View in the US. Hyundai and Kia US accounts can now take surround view captures. I don’t have a US account to test with, so please send a report if it doesn’t work for you. (#22)
  • Search in Navigate. Search now shows you a list of results on the map instead of jumping to the first one. Search a network like “Flo” or “ChargePoint” to see only its chargers. (#28)
  • Trip Planner shows your Arrival SOC in the route header.
  • Trip Planner now skips a slow charger for a fast one just a little further on, and shows each stop’s charging network. (#20)
  • Recent trips show efficiency instead of average speed. (#23)
  • CarPlay: Search shows three categories plus See All Categories, and Recents always keeps two rows.

Fixed

  • Charging history added charges that never happened. A bad reading from the OBD-II adapter right as it connected could show up as a charge from 0%. Those charges are removed automatically. (#25)
  • EU: sign-in said your email or password was wrong when it wasn’t. Since October 22, Hyundai and Kia’s EU sign-in rejects the browser identity Numen was sending. (#26)
  • Navigate stopped finding chargers when you zoomed out or moved the map. (#27)
  • Before you signed in, Navigate searched around downtown Toronto instead of where you are.
  • Preconditioning could show a second countdown before it started. The car takes a little longer to confirm than the firmware waited, so it sent the start again. Fixed in the WiCAN firmware bundled with this build, so tap Update Adapter to get it.
  • With the new firmware, the cluster and HUD no longer show the car’s own preconditioning countdown while Numen is connected, so your CarPlay directions stay on screen. Numen shows the countdown on its CarPlay chip instead.
  • Update Adapter could show for a WiCAN that can’t take an update from the app. It now only appears when your WiCAN is connected and already running the latest Numen firmware.
  • Start Climate from Shortcuts didn’t start the Live Activity.
  • Trip Planner’s arrival estimate could stay on its first guess after the route was refined.
  • CarPlay: Dashboard lane arrows didn’t follow light and dark mode.

Developer

  • Cluster Codes can send the route frame the car uses to recalculate range for a route.

Removed

  • EU: Surround View is hidden while I work out how it works in Europe.

Known Issues

  • EU Surround View: I can’t find how the European apps request a capture. If you’re in Europe and can capture the official Hyundai or Kia app’s traffic while it takes a surround view, please get in touch!
  • EU API issues. European users may still see some inconsistencies with their vehicle status. Please keep sending reports through the app.

October 20, 2026 · Build 20261020.1950

New

  • Command status. A chip above the tab bar follows every command you send: Sent, Busy, Retrying, then Done or Timed Out. Swipe it away anytime.
  • Seats in Climate presets and Quick Start. Heat or cool the driver and passenger seats when climate starts. Available in Canada, the US and Europe on supported vehicles. (#18)
  • Shortcuts. Get Current Status, Fetch Vehicle Status and Start Climate actions. Start Climate can use your last settings or a preset. (#21)
  • Your car on the widget. The home screen widget now shows your model and colour.
  • Signing in. Home now shows “Signing in to Bluelink…” while the app reconnects, and recovers faster if the connection stalls. (#14, #17)
  • Climate presets are hidden while CarPlay is connected.

Fixed

  • Climate presets and Quick Start never turned on the steering wheel heater or rear defrost.
  • Notifications ignored your temperature unit.
  • Range and odometer showed the wrong units for US accounts. (#10, #12, #16)
  • Tapping the charging Live Activity stopped the charge. (#13)
  • Widget and Live Activity buttons didn’t ask for Face ID.
  • The lock screen widget could show unlocked while the app showed locked.
  • EU: lock and unlock flipped back to the wrong state after a command. (#15)
  • Siri: “turn my seat heater off” kept asking for a level.
  • Walk-Away Lock sent two notifications, and reported “Lock Failed” when it had locked.
  • Charging sessions were labelled with where the drive started, not where you parked.
  • Trip Planner: short trips that needed a charge planned no stop and arrived at 0%. It now finds a charger, or tells you when it can’t.
  • CarPlay: lane arrows were the wrong colour after switching between light and dark.
  • CarPlay: turn cards showed the wrong road name on some turns and highway stretches.
  • CarPlay: the route line could shift out of place on the map.
  • CarPlay: alert icons were cut off.

Developer

  • Live Route Line for the Dashboard, in Settings › Developer.
  • Every developer tool is now available in TestFlight builds: OBD Console, OBD Simulators and Bluelink charge controls.
  • Bug reports carry much more detail on commands, status updates and routes, with saved place names removed.

Known Issues

  • Preconditioning can show a second countdown before it starts. A WiCAN firmware update with a longer wait is ready, but hasn’t been sent to anyone yet.
  • EU: some vehicles may still report status inconsistently. Please send a report through the app if you see it.

October 17, 2026 · Build 20261017.1536

Thank you everyone for your patience while I continue to iron out initial connectivity issues with Numen.

New

  • Shake to Report. Shake your phone anywhere in Numen to report a bug. Reports include the logs and device details TestFlight feedback leaves out.
  • ALPHA-TESTING Walk-Away Lock. In Settings › Auto-Lock. Locks the car through the WiCAN as you walk away after a drive, with no Shortcut required.
  • Faster locking. With the WiCAN connected, locks go through it before Bluelink.
  • Seats. The Climate screen shows each seat’s heat and ventilation, and Siri can set them. Just say “Siri, set my seat heater to high in Numen”. Siri defaults to driver location.
  • In Settings › OBD Adapter › Preconditioning Adapter you can now configure the WiCAN directly from Numen without needing to access the web portal.
  • CarPlay: a Battery Preheated chip when the battery is already warm.
  • CarPlay: on the Dashboard, the preconditioning chip sits top left with incidents below it.

Fixed

  • EU: commands failed with “No vehicle selected” after the app had been closed for a while. This was caused by the app not reliably using the refreshToken provided back by Hyundai/Kia.
  • EU: Hyundai lock status is back to how it read before the last build. Some users still may be reporting inaccuracies. Please file a bug through the app directly!!
  • The car image could show the wrong model.
  • Richer error messaging.
  • Charging estimations via OBD-II were wildly inaccurate.
  • Preconditioning started from the car’s own button took a while to show. This latency is now completely eliminated.
  • Some WiCAN OBD-II adapters timed out on every read when using in the OBD port.
  • Bluelink Service in Settings now checks the right server for your region.
  • Range Estimates: short trips now match their “Off by” figure.
  • CarPlay: the arrival card’s text was hard to read after switching between light and dark.

Known Issues

  • Range can read low with an OBD-II adapter after a few short or cold trips. If you see it, shake your phone to send a report. See how you are doing in Settings › Range Estimates. Numen learns as you drive since there is no way to directly query available range via OBD.
  • Siri seat commands may not respond. The Climate screen and the Shortcuts app work.
  • EU API issues. European users may still see inconsistencies with their vehicle status, I’m trying my best to resolve these issues ASAP.
  • EU features: I am aware that European users have access to more controls than NA for example. I am working on including these... Submit a bug report if you notice something that’s missing!

October 15, 2026 · Build 20261015.1027

Fixed

  • EU sign-in failed at the token exchange with HTTP 400, code 9035 ("Unusual activity has been detected"). Since October 13–14, Hyundai and Kia's EU sign-in requires each token request to carry an app signature (X-Fingerprint). Numen now signs the code exchange, token exchange and token refresh.

October 15, 2026 · Build 20261015.0939

New

  • Multiple cars. Every car on the account, switched from the car menu on Home. Trips, charges and parked location are stored per VIN. A paired OBD-II adapter switches to its own car when it connects.
  • CarPlay appearance. Match iPhone (default), Match Car or Always Dark, in CarPlay Settings › Display › Appearance. Match Car follows the head unit's day/night signal, which on the IONIQ 6 comes from the ambient light sensor, not the headlights.
  • Kia Connect. Kia accounts are labelled Kia Connect instead of Bluelink.
  • Europe sign-in. Hyundai and Kia's EU login has rejected the legacy password flow since August 11. Numen now uses the OneApp login.

Fixed

  • Charge times assumed 150 kW at every charger. A 50 kW charger showed 12–80% in 18 minutes; it's over an hour. Times now follow the IONIQ 6's DC curve (235 kW peak), capped at 90% of the charger's rated power. 10–80% at 350 kW is 19 minutes.
  • The Trip Planner picked the furthest charger in range regardless of power. Stops now rank 100 kW+ on a preferred network, then 100 kW+, then slower chargers.
  • Preferred networks were matched on the station name, which Open Charge Map usually sets to the host business ("Walmart"), so Electrify Canada never matched. They're now matched on the network operator.
  • CarPlay charging plans could include CHAdeMO-only and Tesla-only sites. Plans are now limited to CCS1, plus NACS with the adapter.
  • Distance and temperature units were ignored outside a few screens. They now apply to the app, CarPlay, widgets and Live Activities. The CarPlay speed limit converts to mph (labelled MPH), and cluster and HUD distances show mi, or ft under 0.1 mi.
  • Remote climate kept showing as running after a drive when Bluelink couldn't reach the car. It now ends at its set duration, or once the OBD-II adapter has read the car since.
  • Rides in other vehicles were saved as drives and moved the parked location. With an OBD-II adapter or CarPlay set up, a trip only counts if one of them saw the car on during it.
  • Outside Canada, the climate temperature unit was read inverted, so °C showed as °F.
  • Without Bluelink, refresh now polls the OBD-II adapter, and the widget and Watch hide lock, climate and charge.
  • Horn and lights on EU cars returned an error. They're now reported as unsupported.
  • Tires is a menu in Settings instead of a separate page.

October 14, 2026 · Build 20261014.1003

New

  • Early access waitlist. Anyone can install Numen from TestFlight now. Spots open a few at a time, so if they're full when you first open the app, you'll see your place in line. Tap Allow to get a notification when it's your turn, or Try Demo Mode to look around with a sample car while you wait. If you already use Numen, nothing changes for you.
  • Use Numen without Bluelink. Tap Continue Without Bluelink when you sign in, and Numen runs on your OBD-II adapter alone: live SOC, charging, trips and CarPlay navigation. Remote controls need Bluelink. You can sign in any time in Settings › Bluelink Account › Credentials.
  • Range that learns your car. Without Bluelink, range comes from your SOC and how far your car actually goes on a full charge, learned from your trips. A new car starts at 475 km.
  • Climate without Bluelink. The Climate screen shows the cabin temperature from your OBD-II adapter and the temperature outside.
  • Brand and region first. When you sign in, pick your brand and region before entering your account details.

Fixed

  • A trip could show almost the whole battery as used (like 71.8 kWh for a 2% drop) if it started just as the OBD-II adapter connected. Numen now ignores that reading, and trips that already show it are corrected.
  • The Brand and Region pickers now match your theme instead of showing in blue.

October 13, 2026 · Build 20261013.1614

New

  • Smarter range estimates. Arrival SOC now looks at traffic, elevation, weather and your tire install (All-Season vs Winter) along the route, not just distance. It learns from your trips over time. See how it's learning in Settings › Trip Planning › Range Estimates.
  • Tire setting. Tell Numen which tires are on the car. You'll find it in Settings › App Personalization and in CarPlay settings.
  • Edit your stops. Swap a charger, remove a stop or add a new one, in the Trip Planner and in CarPlay. Stops can be any place now, like a coffee shop, not just chargers.
  • More detail on each leg. Every leg of your trip shows its distance, elevation and the weather when you'll get there.
  • No-Refresh Places. Pick places where Numen should leave your car alone, like an underground garage or home. While the car is parked there, Numen won't wake it to check its status. Set them up in Settings › Service.
  • Kia and more regions. Pick your brand and region when you sign in: Hyundai or Kia, in Canada, the US or Europe. We've only tested Hyundai in Canada so far, so if you try another one, please let us know how it goes.

Fixed

  • Place search now finds businesses too, not just street addresses.

Release Notes

What's new in each version of Numen.

Version 1.0

October 15, 2026 · Build 20261015.0939

New

  • More than one car. If your account has more than one car, pick which one you're looking at from the car menu on Home. Trips, charges and the parked spot are kept for each car, and with an OBD-II adapter Numen switches to whichever car you're in.
  • CarPlay appearance. Choose whether CarPlay follows your iPhone's light and dark setting, follows your car, or stays dark. You'll find it in CarPlay under Settings › Display › Appearance.
  • Kia Connect. If you drive a Kia, Numen now calls it Kia Connect instead of Bluelink.
  • Europe sign-in. Hyundai and Kia changed how European accounts sign in, and the old way stopped working in August. Numen now uses the new sign-in.

Fixed

  • The Trip Planner now picks fast chargers first, and only suggests a 50 kW charger when nothing faster is in reach.
  • Your preferred charging networks now count. Electrify Canada stations were being missed, so dealer chargers were suggested instead.
  • Charging times are realistic for each charger. A 50 kW charger used to show 12% to 80% in 18 minutes; it's over an hour.
  • CarPlay only plans stops at chargers your car can plug into, and skips Tesla sites that don't let other cars charge.
  • Miles and °F now apply everywhere: the app, CarPlay, widgets and Live Activities. With miles selected, the CarPlay speed limit shows mph, and directions in the cluster and HUD show miles and feet.
  • Climate no longer shows as running hours after a drive.
  • A ride in a taxi or someone else's car is no longer saved as one of your drives, and no longer moves where your car is parked.
  • Outside Canada, climate temperatures were read as Fahrenheit when they were Celsius.
  • Without Bluelink, refreshing reads your OBD-II adapter, and the widget and Watch hide the lock, climate and charge buttons.
  • Horn and lights now say they aren't available for European cars, instead of failing.
  • The tire setting is now a quick menu instead of its own page.

October 14, 2026 · Build 20261014.1003

New

  • Early access waitlist. Anyone can install Numen from TestFlight now. Spots open a few at a time, so if they're full when you first open the app, you'll see your place in line. Tap Allow to get a notification when it's your turn, or Try Demo Mode to look around with a sample car while you wait. If you already use Numen, nothing changes for you.
  • Use Numen without Bluelink. Tap Continue Without Bluelink when you sign in, and Numen runs on your OBD-II adapter alone: live SOC, charging, trips and CarPlay navigation. Remote controls need Bluelink. You can sign in any time in Settings › Bluelink Account › Credentials.
  • Range that learns your car. Without Bluelink, range comes from your SOC and how far your car actually goes on a full charge, learned from your trips. A new car starts at 475 km.
  • Climate without Bluelink. The Climate screen shows the cabin temperature from your OBD-II adapter and the temperature outside.
  • Brand and region first. When you sign in, pick your brand and region before entering your account details.

Fixed

  • A trip could show almost the whole battery as used (like 71.8 kWh for a 2% drop) if it started just as the OBD-II adapter connected. Numen now ignores that reading, and trips that already show it are corrected.
  • The Brand and Region pickers now match your theme instead of showing in blue.

October 13, 2026 · Build 20261013.1614

New

  • Smarter range estimates. Arrival SOC now looks at traffic, elevation, weather and your tire install (All-Season vs Winter) along the route, not just distance. It learns from your trips over time. See how it's learning in Settings › Trip Planning › Range Estimates.
  • Tire setting. Tell Numen which tires are on the car. You'll find it in Settings › App Personalization and in CarPlay settings.
  • Edit your stops. Swap a charger, remove a stop or add a new one, in the Trip Planner and in CarPlay. Stops can be any place now, like a coffee shop, not just chargers.
  • More detail on each leg. Every leg of your trip shows its distance, elevation and the weather when you'll get there.
  • No-Refresh Places. Pick places where Numen should leave your car alone, like an underground garage or home. While the car is parked there, Numen won't wake it to check its status. Set them up in Settings › Service.
  • Kia and more regions. Pick your brand and region when you sign in: Hyundai or Kia, in Canada, the US or Europe. We've only tested Hyundai in Canada so far, so if you try another one, please let us know how it goes.

Fixed

  • Place search now finds businesses too, not just street addresses.

Initial Release

The first version of Numen, available through TestFlight early access.

Release Notes

What's new in each version of Numen.

Version 1.0

October 13, 2026 · Build 20261013.1614

New

  • Smarter range estimates. Arrival charge now looks at traffic, hills, weather and your tires along the route, not just distance. It learns from your trips over time. See how it's doing in Settings › Trip Planning › Range Estimates.
  • Tires. Choose all-season, winter or summer tires in Settings › App Personalization or in CarPlay settings.
  • Edit stops. Change, remove or add stops in the Trip Planner and in CarPlay. You can add any place as a stop, not just chargers.
  • Leg details. Each leg of a trip shows its distance, how much it climbs, and the weather when you get there.
  • No-Refresh Places. Add places like a parking garage or home, and Numen won't wake your car to check on it while it's parked there. Find it in Settings › Service.
  • Kia and more regions. Choose your brand and region when you sign in: Hyundai or Kia, in Canada, the US or Europe. Kia and regions outside Canada haven't been tested yet, so reports are especially welcome.

Fixed

  • Searching for a place now finds businesses, not just street addresses.

Initial Release

The first version of Numen, available through TestFlight early access.

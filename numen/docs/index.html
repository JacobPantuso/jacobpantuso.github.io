How Numen Talks to Your Car

Numen combines up to three sources. Bluelink is all you need, and each adapter adds more, with the freshest reading always shown.

SourceWorks FromUsed For
BluelinkAnywhereRemote commands and the car's last reported status
OBD-II AdapterAt the carLive charge, charge power, locks, doors, trips and regen. Required for CarPlay.
Harness AdapterAt the carBattery preconditioning, and directions on the cluster and HUD

Status

Early BetaNumen is in early beta on TestFlight, built and tested on an IONIQ 6 with a Canadian Bluelink account. Other e-GMP vehicles and regions may work, and reports from them are especially valuable.
